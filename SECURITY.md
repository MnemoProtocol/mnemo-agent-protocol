# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities in Mnemo privately to
**security@trymnemo.com**.

Please do not open a public GitHub issue, pull request or discussion for a
security report, and please do not disclose the issue publicly until we have
had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate it.

## What to include

A useful report usually contains:

- the affected component (API endpoint, SDK version, protocol document or website page);
- a description of the issue and its potential impact;
- step-by-step reproduction instructions, with any proof-of-concept limited to what is needed to show the issue;
- the time of testing and any request identifiers you observed;
- how you would like to be credited, if at all.

## Testing guidelines

This policy does not authorize destructive or disruptive testing. In
particular, do not:

- access, modify or delete data that does not belong to you;
- degrade or interrupt the service (including denial-of-service or volume testing);
- use social engineering, phishing or physical attacks;
- make payments, settlements or other financial transactions beyond what you
  would make as an ordinary customer, or attempt to obtain service without paying;
- test third-party services that Mnemo relies on.

If you encounter personal data or credentials while investigating, stop, do
not retain them, and tell us in your report.

## What to expect

We aim to acknowledge valid reports within 5 business days. We will work with
you to understand and address the issue, and keep you informed of material
progress where practicable. We do not currently offer a monetary bug bounty.

This policy does not grant any license or legal authorization beyond what it
expressly states.
